Getting started (one time)

  1. Plug in the mixer's USB cable, the song drive, and the TV before opening The Butcher Karaoke.
  2. Open The Butcher Karaoke from the desktop.
  3. The first time, the Song Folders box opens. Click + Add Folder, pick the song drive (or the karaoke folder on it), and wait while it reads the songs. On a big drive this takes a few minutes, only the first time. Then click Done.
  4. At the top, next to Sound to:, pick the mixer. That sends the music to the mixer.
  5. Next to Record from:, pick the mixer too. The little bar next to it moves when someone talks into the mic.

The app remembers all of this. Next time, just open it.

Song folders and the drive

Click 📁 Song Folders at the top.

  • + Add Folder adds another folder or drive. All songs show together in one list.
  • Check for New Songs looks for songs that were just copied on. The app also does this by itself every time it opens.
  • Remove takes a folder out of the list. It does not delete any songs.
  • 🟢 means connected. ⚪ means that drive isn't plugged in.
  • File names look like: if many song names show backwards (the singer where the title should be), switch this.

If the drive isn't plugged in, a red ⚠ Not plugged in note shows at the top and those songs show in red. Plug it in and they come back within about 15 seconds. Any USB port works.

The song list includes MP3+G zip files, MP3s with a matching CDG file, MP4 videos, and plain MP3s (marked "Audio only": music but no words on the TV). Old AVI, MPG and WMA files aren't listed because they can't play.

Finding songs

  • Type any part of the song name, the singer, or the disc number (like SC8123) in the search box on the left. More words narrow it down, for example patsy crazy.
  • Sort: Artist / Title changes the order of the list.
  • Add (or double-clicking the song) puts it on Tonight's Playlist on the right. 📋+ adds it to a saved playlist instead (see Saved playlists).

Singing a song

Tonight's Playlist (on the right) is the list for the show. Click ▶ next to a song on it to start that song. The words show on the TV.

The buttons at the bottom:

  • ▶ / ❚❚ play or pause.
  • ■ stops the song.
  • ⏭ plays the next song on Tonight's Playlist.
  • Click anywhere on the long bar to jump to that part of the song.
  • The 🔊 slider is the volume.
  • Auto-play next song: when checked, the next song starts by itself when one finishes.

On Tonight's Playlist, ▲ ▼ move a song up or down, and ✕ takes it off. Songs that have been sung get a ✓ and a line through them. Clear Sung removes those, and Clear All empties the list. The list is kept even when the computer is turned off.

Changing the key

While a song is playing, press ♯ + to raise it or ♭ − to lower it, one step at a time (up to 6 each way). The speed stays the same.

The key is remembered for that song, shown as Key +2 and so on (0 is the original key), and it's kept when the list is saved as a playlist.

A few video songs can't change key; the buttons are greyed out for those.

Saved playlists

A saved playlist is a list of songs to use again, like Elvis, E Gospel or Merle Hag. Click the Playlists tab (next to Song Library).

  • Add all to tonight puts the whole list on Tonight's Playlist, ready to sing.
  • Open shows its songs. In there, Add puts one song on Tonight's Playlist, ▲ ▼ move songs, ✕ takes a song off the saved list, and Rename changes the list's name. ‹ All Playlists goes back.
  • To make a new one: put songs on Tonight's Playlist and click 💾 Save as Playlist, or click + New Playlist and add songs with 📋+ in the Song Library.
  • 🗑 deletes a saved playlist. The songs themselves are not deleted.
  • Import from Show Presenter Pro (shown only when Show Presenter is on the computer) brings in its lists, named the same way they were there.

The TV screen

If the TV is connected when the app opens, the words show on it automatically, full screen.

  • If the TV screen isn't showing, click 📺 TV Screen at the top.
  • Double-click the TV picture to switch full screen on or off.
  • Between songs, the TV shows the next song.
  • If the TV cable comes out, the TV screen closes so it doesn't cover the controls. Plug it back in and it comes back by itself.

Recording

  1. Check that the little bar next to Record from: moves when someone talks into the mic.
  2. Click ● REC so it turns red and says REC ON.
  3. Play a song. It records from start to finish, with ● REC and a timer next to the song name.

The recording saves itself when the song ends, is stopped, or the next one starts. Every song played while REC is on gets recorded; click ● REC ON again to stop recording. If the app is closed during a recording, it saves the recording first.

The app builds the recording from the mic (from the mixer) plus a clean copy of the music, in the key that was picked.

Voice too quiet or too loud? Click 🎚 Rec Mix next to REC. Move 🎤 Mic up to make the voice louder, or 🎵 Music down to make the music quieter. These only change the recording, not what the room hears, and they're remembered. Reset to normal puts them back. If the voice sounds a little ahead of or behind the music, move ⏱ Timing a small step and try again.

New recordings go into Documents › Karaoke Recordings, named with the song and the date, unless a project is picked next to New recordings go to: (see Projects and CDs).

Listening to recordings

Click the Recordings tab. At the top, Project: shows either Not in a project yet (loose recordings, newest first) or a project's songs in CD order.

  • ▶ plays it; ■ stops it.
  • ✏️ renames it, for example from "How Great Thou Art - 2026-11-25 7.42pm" to "How Great Thou Art".
  • 💿 moves it into a project (a CD), or back out.
  • 📂 shows the file in its folder, for copying or sending it to family.
  • 🗑 deletes it. It goes to the Recycle Bin, so it can be brought back.
  • Open Folder (top right) opens the folder for what's shown.

Projects and CDs

A project is a group of recordings that will become a CD, like Gospel CD. Its songs are numbered in the order they'll play on the CD.

  1. In the Recordings tab, click + New Project and type a name.
  2. Next to New recordings go to:, pick that project. Every song recorded from then on goes straight into it as the next track, named by the song's title.
  3. To add recordings made earlier, pick Not in a project yet, click 💿 next to a recording, and pick the project.
  4. With the project picked, ▲ ▼ change the track order. The line under the buttons shows how long the CD will be; one CD holds up to 80 minutes.
  5. When it's ready, click 💿 Burn CD. The app looks for the USB burner and asks for a blank CD-R. Put one in, and when it says Ready to burn, click Burn CD. A bar shows each song being written. Don't unplug the burner or close the app until it says Done; the CD pops out by itself.

Use CD-R discs: CD-RW discs don't play in many car stereos, and a DVD won't work as a music CD. The finished CD plays in any CD player, car or home.

Rename and 🗑 next to the project name rename or delete the whole project. Each project is a normal folder inside Documents › Karaoke Recordings, so its songs can also be copied to a USB stick or sent to family.

If something goes wrong

Click ⚠ Report a Problem at the top. Type what happened if you can (it's okay to leave it blank) and click Send Report. It goes to Camby with the details needed to fix it. If the button has turned red, the app noticed a problem by itself; please send a report.

No sound: check that Sound to: is set to the mixer, the mixer is on, its USB cable is plugged in, and the 🔊 slider is turned up.

The mixer isn't in the list: plug its USB cable in, wait a few seconds, and look again. If it still isn't there, close the app and open it again.

No words on the TV: click 📺 TV Screen, and check that the TV is on and set to the right input.

A song won't play: a message says why. Try another version of the song if there is one, and send a report.

The app closed or froze: open it again; Tonight's Playlist is kept. If it asks to send a report, click Send report.

Updates

The app updates itself. New versions download in the background, and a yellow bar at the top says when one is ready. It installs the next time the app is closed, so it never interrupts a song. Restart and update now installs it right away.
