Getting started
1. Click 📁 Song Folders → + Add Folder and pick the karaoke drive. The first read of a big drive takes a few minutes; after that the app opens instantly and picks up new songs by itself.
2. Under Sound to, pick the mixer.
3. With the TV plugged in, click 📺 TV Screen. It opens full screen on the TV. Double-click it to switch full screen on or off.
4. Search for a song and click Add, then click ▶ on the playlist to sing it.

Plays MP3+G zip files, matching .mp3 + .cdg files, and MP4 karaoke videos.

Recording: pick the mixer under Record from and turn on ● REC. Every song played while it's on is saved as an MP3 in Documents › Karaoke Recordings.
