Getting started (one time)

  1. Plug in the mixer's USB cable and the song drive, and connect the TV, before opening Karaoke Player.
  2. Open Karaoke Player from the desktop.
  3. The first time, the Song Folders box opens. Click + Add Folder, pick the song drive (or the karaoke folder on it), and wait while it reads the songs. On a big drive this takes a few minutes, only the first time. Then click Done.
  4. At the top, next to Sound to:, pick the mixer. That sends the music to the mixer.
  5. Next to Record from:, pick the mixer too. The little bar next to it moves when someone talks into the mic.

The app remembers all of this. Next time, just open it.

Singing a song

  1. Type part of the song or the singer's name in the search box on the left, for example patsy crazy.
  2. Click Add next to the song. It goes onto the Playlist on the right.
  3. Click ▶ next to the song on the Playlist to start it. The words show on the TV.

The buttons at the bottom:

  • ▶ / ❚❚ play or pause. The space bar does the same.
  • ■ stops the song.
  • ⏭ plays the next song on the Playlist.
  • Click anywhere on the long bar to jump to that part of the song.
  • The 🔊 slider is the volume.
  • Auto-play next song: when checked, the next song starts by itself when one finishes.

On the Playlist, ▲ ▼ move a song up or down, and ✕ takes it off. Songs that have been sung get a ✓ and a line through them. Clear Sung removes those; Clear All empties the list. The Playlist is saved, even if the computer is turned off.

Changing the key

While a song is playing, press ♯ + to raise it or ♭ − to lower it, one step at a time (up to 6 each way). The speed stays the same.

The app remembers the key for that song on the Playlist, shown as Key +2 and so on. 0 is the original key.

A few video songs can't change key; the buttons are greyed out for those.

The TV screen

If the TV is connected when the app opens, the words show on it automatically, full screen.

  • If the TV screen isn't showing, click 📺 TV Screen at the top.
  • Double-click the TV picture to switch full screen on or off.
  • Between songs, the TV shows who's Up next.
  • If the TV cable comes out, the TV screen closes so it doesn't cover the controls. Plug it back in and it comes back by itself.

Recording

  1. Make sure Record from: is set to the mixer and the little bar moves when someone talks into the mic.
  2. Click ● REC so it turns red and says REC ON.
  3. Play a song. It records from start to finish. You'll see ● REC and a timer next to the song name.

When the song ends, is stopped, or the next one starts, the recording is saved by itself. Every song played while REC is on gets recorded. Click ● REC ON again to turn recording off.

The recording is what the mixer hears: the singer and the music together. If the voice is too quiet in the recording, turn the music down a little on the mixer.

Recordings are saved in Documents › Karaoke Recordings, named with the song and the date.

Listening to recordings

Click the Recordings tab (next to Song Library). Newest recordings are at the top.

  • ▶ plays it; ■ stops it.
  • 📂 shows the file in its folder, for copying or sending it to family.
  • 🗑 deletes it. It goes to the Recycle Bin, so it can still be brought back.
  • Open Folder opens the whole recordings folder.

Song folders and the drive

Click 📁 Song Folders at the top.

  • + Add Folder adds another folder or drive. All songs show together in one list.
  • Check for New Songs looks for songs that were just copied on. The app also does this by itself every time it opens.
  • Remove takes a folder out of the list. It does not delete any songs.
  • 🟢 means connected. ⚪ means that drive isn't plugged in.

If the drive isn't plugged in, a red ⚠ Not plugged in note shows at the top and those songs are marked. Plug it in and they come back within a few seconds. It doesn't matter which USB port you use.

Songs that play: MP3+G zip files, MP3 with a matching CDG file, and MP4 videos. Old AVI or MPG videos won't play.

Finding songs

  • Type any part of the song name, the singer, or the disc number (like SC8123). More words narrow it down.
  • Sort: Artist / Title changes the order of the list.
  • Double-click a song to add it to the Playlist.
  • If song names show backwards (singer where the title should be), open 📁 Song Folders and change File names look like.

Bigger text

Hold Ctrl and press + to make everything bigger, Ctrl and − to make it smaller, and Ctrl and 0 to go back to normal. Or press Alt and use the View menu.

If something goes wrong

Click ⚠ Report a Problem at the top. Type what happened if you can (it's okay to leave it blank) and click Send Report. It goes to Camby with the details needed to fix it. If the button turns red, the app noticed a problem by itself; please send a report.

No sound: check Sound to: is set to the mixer, the mixer is on, and its USB cable is plugged in. The volume slider at the bottom should be turned up.

The mixer isn't in the list: plug its USB cable in, wait a few seconds, and look again. If it still isn't there, close the app and open it again.

No words on the TV: click 📺 TV Screen. Check the TV is on and set to the right input.

A song won't play: a message says why. Try another version of the song if there is one, and send a report.

The app closed or froze: open it again. The Playlist is saved. If it asks to send a report, click Send report.

When the app closes during a recording, it saves the recording first. That can take a few seconds.

Updates

The app updates itself. New versions download in the background. A yellow bar at the top says when one is ready, and it installs the next time the app is closed, so it never interrupts a song. Click Restart and update now to install it right away.
